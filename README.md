| **Specialty Line** | 3-5 days | 290+ | ✅ Accepted | Food, cosmetics, supplements |

> 💡 **Key insight**: If you're shipping food, tea, cosmetics, or supplements, regular carriers will reject your package. A dedicated cross-strait sensitive goods line is the only viable option.

## Prohibited Items

### Absolutely Cannot Ship
- Fresh fruits, vegetables, raw meat
- Live animals or plants
- Weapons, ammunition, explosives
- Counterfeit goods, pirated media
- Narcotics and controlled substances

### Requires Special Handling (Sensitive Goods)
- Food, snacks, tea leaves
- Cosmetics, skincare products
- Health supplements, vitamins
- Electronics with built-in batteries
- Liquids over 100ml

## Sensitive Goods Guide

When shipping sensitive goods, you need a carrier that offers:
- **Dual customs clearance** (Taiwan export + China import)
- **Tax-included service** (no surprise fees at customs)
- **Category-based pricing** (not one-size-fits-all)

For a real-world guide on what can and cannot be shipped,
see [Subao Logistics — Taiwan to China Shipping Service](https://subao.tw).

## FAQ

### Q: Can I ship pineapple cakes to China?
**A:** Yes, factory-sealed packaged foods are fine. Fresh/homemade items without proper packaging are prohibited.

### Q: How long does shipping take?
**A:** General cargo 3-5 working days, sensitive goods 5-7 days, large volume 10-15 days.

### Q: Will my package be taxed?
**A:** Personal use items (under reasonable quantity) are typically tax-free. Commercial quantities may incur 10-15% import tax.

### Q: How do I pack sensitive goods?
**A:** Original packaging preferred. Liquids must be sealed in leak-proof bags. Cosmetics wrapped in bubble wrap. Keep general and sensitive items in separate boxes.
