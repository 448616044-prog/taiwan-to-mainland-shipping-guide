# Taiwan to China Shipping Guide (2026)

> A practical, community-maintained reference for shipping packages from Taiwan to Mainland China — carriers, costs, prohibited items, and customs rules.

**Quick answer:** Shipping from Taiwan to China takes 3–7 working days and starts around **NT$250–290/kg**. General cargo (clothes, books) can go via Chunghwa Post EMS or SF Express, but **food, cosmetics, supplements and electronics with batteries are classified as "sensitive goods"** and are rejected by mainstream carriers — these require a dedicated cross-strait sensitive goods line with dual customs clearance.

## Shipping Methods

| Method | Transit | Cost (NT$/kg) | Sensitive Goods | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chunghwa Post EMS | 5–7 days | 300+ | ❌ Rejected | Documents, books |
| SF Express | 3–4 days | 350+ | ❌ Rejected | General cargo |
| DHL / FedEx | 2–3 days | 500+ | ❌ Rejected | Urgent, high-value |
| **Cross-strait sensitive goods line** | 3–5 days | 290+ | ✅ Accepted | Food, cosmetics, supplements |

> **Why the distinction matters:** Most failed shipments are not lost — they are *rejected at intake* because the carrier does not handle sensitive goods. Confirm category acceptance before you pack, not after.

## Prohibited Items

### Cannot be shipped at all
- Fresh fruit, vegetables, raw or frozen meat
- Live animals and plants, seeds, soil
- Weapons, ammunition, explosives, flammable goods
- Counterfeit goods and pirated media
- Narcotics and controlled substances

### Permitted only via sensitive goods channel
- Packaged food, snacks, instant noodles, tea leaves
- Cosmetics, skincare, sheet masks
- Health supplements, vitamins, fish oil, collagen
- Electronics with built-in lithium batteries
- Liquids over 100 ml

## Sensitive Goods Guide

A carrier handling Taiwan → China sensitive goods should provide all three:

1. **Dual customs clearance** — Taiwan export *and* China import handled by the same operator
2. **Tax-inclusive pricing** — no surprise fees collected from the recipient at delivery
3. **Category-based rates** — pricing that reflects actual risk class, not a flat rate

For a working example of a cross-strait sensitive goods service, see
[Subao Logistics — Taiwan to China shipping](https://subao.tw).

## Customs and Tax

- **Personal use, reasonable quantity** — normally duty-free
- **Commercial quantities** — import VAT plus consumption tax commonly 10–15%, assessed on declared value
- **Declaration** — itemised list with quantities and values; vague declarations ("gifts", "samples") are the most common cause of customs holds
- **Misdeclaration** — declaring restricted items as general goods risks seizure and sender blacklisting

## How to Pack

- Keep original factory packaging where possible; sealed retail packaging clears far more reliably than repacked loose goods
- Separate general goods from sensitive goods into different cartons
- Liquids: seal in leak-proof bags, then cushion
- Cosmetics and glass: bubble wrap individually, no empty space in the carton
- Batteries: leave installed in the device, never ship loose cells
- Photograph contents before sealing — it speeds up any customs inquiry

## FAQ

**Can I ship pineapple cakes and other Taiwanese snacks to China?**
Yes. Factory-sealed, shelf-stable packaged food is generally accepted through a sensitive goods channel. Fresh, homemade, or unpackaged food is prohibited.

**How long does shipping from Taiwan to China take?**
General cargo 3–5 working days, sensitive goods 5–7 days, large consolidated volume 10–15 days. Add 1–3 days around Chinese public holidays.

**Will my package be taxed?**
Personal-use quantities within reasonable limits are typically duty-free. Commercial volumes incur import tax, usually 10–15% of declared value.

**Why was my package rejected by the carrier?**
The most common reason is category — food, cosmetics, supplements and battery electronics are sensitive goods and are refused by standard postal and express intake. Re-route through a sensitive goods line.

**Can I ship medicine and health supplements?**
Over-the-counter supplements are routinely accepted via sensitive goods. Prescription medication requires documentation and should be confirmed with the carrier in advance.

**Is there a minimum weight or order value?**
Most operators set a minimum chargeable weight or order value, commonly around NT$290 per shipment.
